# Country silhouette overrides

These files replace low-detail Natural Earth 10m outlines that are not fair at Puzzler's card size.

- `mc.geojson` — Monaco (MCO), geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM0, source: OpenStreetMap and Wambacher.
- `va.geojson` — Vatican City (VAT), geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM0, source: geoBoundaries and Wikimedia Commons.

Both files are from geoBoundaries release `9469f09` and are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://www.geoboundaries.org/countryDownloads.html). Puzzler must retain visible geoBoundaries attribution.
