# Country silhouette overrides

These files replace low-detail Natural Earth 10m outlines that are not fair at Puzzler's card size. For the Marshall Islands, the game uses its central chain of atolls at their real relative positions, rather than spreading the whole country across its ocean-wide extent.

- `bb.geojson` — Barbados (BRB), geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM0, source: geoBoundaries and Wikimedia Commons.
- `mc.geojson` — Monaco (MCO), geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM0, source: OpenStreetMap and Wambacher.
- `mh.geojson` — Marshall Islands (MHL), geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM0, source: OpenStreetMap and Wambacher.
- `nr.geojson` — Nauru (NRU), geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM0, source: OpenStreetMap and Wambacher.
- `sm.geojson` — San Marino (SMR), geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM0, source: OpenStreetMap and Wambacher.
- `tv.geojson` — Tuvalu (TUV), geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM0, source: OpenStreetMap and Wambacher.
- `va.geojson` — Vatican City (VAT), geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM0, source: geoBoundaries and Wikimedia Commons.

All files are from geoBoundaries release `9469f09` and are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://www.geoboundaries.org/countryDownloads.html). Puzzler must retain visible geoBoundaries attribution.
